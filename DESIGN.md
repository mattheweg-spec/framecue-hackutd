# FrameCue — Signal Studies

An original graphic direction combining industrial science-fiction interfaces, ZeroDay's hacking context, and loose animation-inspired editorial energy. No characters, franchise logos, or reproduced artwork.

## Visual rules

- Ink `#101111` and graphite `#191b1b` form the equipment panels. Warm paper `#eeeadd` distinguishes destination entry.
- Acid yellow `#dfef58` means a primary action or perception status. Cyan `#76dcd7` identifies route cues. Vermilion `#ff6354` identifies obstacle cues. Text and location distinguish these channels in addition to color.
- Condensed display typography carries the headline and wordmark. Segoe UI carries instructions; Consolas carries instrument labels. Use installed fonts, with no runtime font download.
- Original vector signal strokes, registration marks, narrow rules, and restrained stippling provide character. Avoid perpetual glitching, moving scanlines, decorative live telemetry, and glow on every element.
- The wearable is the first panel in the right column. Camera and routing occupy the left. Technical explanations and logs expand on request.
- Tracking brackets maintain their dimensions across priority changes. Hazard state changes color and label emphasis, never padding or control-button styling.
- Responsive layout collapses below 800 px. Controls wrap; labels never rely on ellipsis. Reduced-motion preferences disable transitions.

## Assets and implementation

`framecue.css` owns presentation. `assets/signal-study.svg` is original vector art. Existing interaction IDs and event handlers remain the interface contract. CAD mesh geometry and its articulation are unchanged; the viewer uses a warmer neutral light rig.

Do not describe the simulation as tested physical haptics or a validated navigation aid. Do not use the older gallery OBJ views to imply that the later ankle cuff is shown.
